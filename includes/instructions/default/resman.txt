<h1>Task overview</h1>

<p>In the resource management task, participants must limit leakage from the two main tanks (A and B) by using a set of backup tanks and pumps, each with its own specific power.</p>

<p>The participant's task is to turn the numbered pumps on and off so that tanks A and B stay as close as possible to their normal levels.</p>

<p>Pumps may occasionally fail.</p>
