<h1>Task overview</h1>

<p>At the top right of the screen, the participant has a scheduler that shows, for each task (represented by a capital letter and a timeline), the phases during which it will be active.</p>

<p>There is also an automation system that can share the workload with the participant. When automation is active for a given task, the scheduler indicates it.</p>

<p>This helps the participant manage effort and allocate resources efficiently.</p>
